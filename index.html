Offline demo delivery
Native iOS/Android test apps: receiver opens Receive, sender scans the account QR once and confirms. Bluetooth delivers the signed offer. Receiver checks and accepts; its signed acknowledgement returns automatically. Both apps must stay open with Bluetooth on. A dropped acknowledgement leaves sender Pending; retry the saved transaction.
Safari/PWA or QR recovery steps:
  1. Receiver opens Receive. Sender scans their account QR.
  2. Sender enters an amount and confirms. Funds are reserved; status is Pending.
  3. Receiver scans sender's payment QR and accepts or rejects. Receiver credit occurs only on acceptance.
  4. Sender scans receiver's receipt QR. Matching signature, amount and transaction finalize sender status and balance.
No NFC or background delivery. Native Bluetooth is a foreground test path; Safari/PWA uses the QR exchange above. Reconnect reconciles signed receipts already imported on this account; no cloud server is connected. Lost receipt? Receiver can reopen that transaction and show its saved receipt again. A pending sender cannot cancel unilaterally because the receiver may already have accepted. Online mode uses the same manual QR exchange. Local storage is not a trusted financial ledger; tampering, copying or restoring app data can break demo guarantees.
