Confirm creates a signed payment QR and reserves demo funds. Status stays Pending until this device verifies the receiver's signed receipt. There is no automatic online server delivery.


Offline QR exchange
  1. Receiver opens Receive. Sender scans their account QR.
  2. Sender enters an amount and confirms. Funds are reserved; status is Pending.
  3. Receiver scans sender's payment QR and accepts or rejects. Receiver credit occurs only on acceptance.
  4. Sender scans receiver's receipt QR. Matching signature, amount and transaction finalize sender status and balance.
No Bluetooth, NFC, background transport or reconnect synchronization. Lost receipt? Receiver can reopen that transaction and show its saved receipt again. A pending sender cannot cancel unilaterally because the receiver may already have accepted. Online mode uses the same manual QR exchange. Local storage is not a trusted financial ledger; tampering, copying or restoring app data can break demo guarantees.
