Version 3.1 · Automatic recipient details and offline transfer steps
  1. Install or update the Android APK. On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Existing iPhone users: reopen online until Version 3.1.0 · Offline ready appears.
  2. Create or log in to your local account with an 8–12 digit PIN. OTP/backend service is not configured: phone numbers remain Unverified. Open Receive Money to see your personal QR. Each browser/PWA or APK has its own persistent account. Use the same installation next time; clearing site data or uninstalling can remove the account and receipts. No recovery or cross-device login is connected.
  3. Sender scans that account QR: name and full Account ID fill automatically. Unknown names are marked unverified; independently compare with the owner and confirm the checkbox. Enter the demo amount, review and confirm. Status stays Pending and funds are reserved.
  4. Receiver scans sender's new payment QR to actually receive the transaction, then accepts or rejects. This scan is delivery, not a duplicate account scan. Receiver shows the resulting receipt QR; sender scans it to finalize the matching transaction.
  5. Both devices must prepare the app online once before a PWA offline cold launch. The Android APK bundles its files. QR generation, signing and decoding then need no internet. There is no Bluetooth transport or cloud sync. Reconnect rechecks only signed receipts imported on that logged-in account. Unknown recipients require an independently checked local contact record; QR names alone are not trusted. Online mode also uses manual QR exchange.

Tap Test sound for the bundled offline chime. If unheard, check media volume/output and iPhone Silent/Focus settings. Browser restrictions are shown; the app cannot detect or override the silent switch.

Demo ledger only. Signed public details do not verify personal identity, real funds or banking settlement. Safeguards cannot prevent tampering, cloning or restoration of local app data.
